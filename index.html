How this works

The name is terrain: f = 1 − 0.62 G₁∗L − 0.30 G₂∗L + η, blurred letter masks carved into a random field η whose Fourier coefficients follow Ornstein–Uhlenbeck processes. Water fills the sublevel set {f ≤ t}, the pixel filtration my flood detection paper runs on radar images, and the inset is its persistence diagram, recomputed every frame by union-find: ● pools (H₀), ○ islands (H₁), β their counts at the current level. Drag across the name to dig channels and rings.
