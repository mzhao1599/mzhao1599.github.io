Somnio Robotics

2026 DN Hacks 2026 · with two teammates

Somnio keeps a robot’s vision-language-action policy (SmolVLA) frozen and adapts it to a changed scene by tuning a small parameter file offline, in unattended “sleep” cycles: 17 numbers covering timing, a speed cap, the gripper, a homing move and approach shaping, or 21 once four camera re-framing numbers are added. A cross-entropy-method search proposes new files, and a gate on a separate set of layouts decides whether one replaces the current version. The policy’s weights never change: no fine-tuning, demonstrations or human labels.

In LIBERO simulation, shifting the arm’s starting joints by 0.1 rad drops task success from 80% to 22%; one sleep cycle brings it back to 54% and a second to 70% on held-out layouts. A 6° camera tilt drops it from 88% to 14%, and one cycle recovers 58%. Several other tasks recovered only partly or not at all, and the write-up reports those too.

Python · SmolVLA · LIBERO-Plus · cross-entropy method

Topology-informed flood detection

2025 U.S. Naval Research Laboratory · with Sophia Li, Raghu G. Raj and Tianyu Chen

Detects floods in time series of Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 optical images. Each image is swept by a sublevel-set filtration on its pixels, and the resulting persistence diagram (which regions appear, merge and enclose holes as the threshold rises) becomes a 200-number feature, about a tenth the size of the ResNet-50’s 2048. That feature is fed, alone or alongside the CNN features, into a GRU that labels every frame of the sequence.

On the SEN12-FLOOD benchmark, the paper reports 0.989 frame-level accuracy for the fused model with both sensors, against 0.974 for ResNet50-GRU on the same input and 0.957 previously reported.

Python · PyTorch · CubicalRipser · Sentinel-1/2

Qudoku: quantum-inspired optimization

2024–25 George Mason University · with Fei Li

Writes Sudoku and MaxCut as “find the lowest-energy state of interacting spins” (a QUBO, then an Ising Hamiltonian) and solves them on a laptop by simulating a slow quantum anneal with a matrix product state and DMRG, in Julia with ITensors. A Sudoku grid is 729 yes/no variables; for the demo puzzle, its 23 clues leave 232 spins.

The Sudoku anneal reaches the target energy and prints a valid board in about three minutes. On the 80-vertex MaxCut instance pm1s_80.0 it finds a cut of 79, the optimum listed in the Biq Mac library. The paper reports MaxCut instances with up to 251 nodes and 3,265 edges.

Julia · ITensors · matrix product states · DMRG

Paper · Quantum Information & Computation 25 (2025) ↗GitHub ↗

Virtuoso

2026

A social practice tracker for musicians, like Strava for practice sessions: a timer that logs breaks, follows and follow requests, a feed with kudos and comments, streaks, a practice calendar and a leaderboard. While the timer runs, an AudioWorklet keeps a 30-second ring buffer of the microphone, so one click saves a good take after you have played it. Row-level security in Postgres decides who can see a private account’s sessions.
