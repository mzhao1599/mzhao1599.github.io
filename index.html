Stanford · computer science + mathematics

Max Zhao

I study computer science and mathematics at Stanford. I like building systems that turn complex ideas and data into something people can use — from flood response software to machine learning and optimization research.

In 2026, I built a flood response platform through Palantir’s Valley Forge Grant. Previously, I worked on satellite imagery models for flood detection at the U.S. Naval Research Lab and on quantum-inspired optimization at George Mason University. Outside of research and code, I play violin.

Research & recognition

Flood response software. Independent product work supported by Palantir’s Valley Forge Grant; built an interface and trigger-based decision engine for emergency response workflows.

Flood detection. ML research internship at the U.S. Naval Research Lab. Co-author of Topology-Informed Neural Networks for Flood Detection in Optical and Synthetic Aperture Radar Imagery; the fused model reached 98.9% accuracy on SEN12-FLOOD, against 95.7% previously reported.

Optimization. First-author work on quantum-inspired algorithms at George Mason University, published in Quantum Information and Computation (arXiv).
